Dear Teacher: You Deserve to Enjoy the Trip, Too

Jul 22, 2025 · By Tony Cimino-Johnson · 3 min read

Why your presence matters — and so does your joy.

As a former teacher, I know the feeling.

You’ve planned every detail. You’ve chased down permission slips. You’ve answered late-night emails. You’ve coordinated with admin, parents, chaperones, and 30 high-energy teenagers. And when the bus finally pulls out of the school parking lot, you don’t exhale — you brace yourself.

But I’m here to tell you something that took me far too long to believe: you deserve to enjoy the trip, too.

Yes, you’re the leader. Yes, you’re responsible. But that doesn’t mean you can’t be present, inspired, and even a little bit awed by the experience you helped create.

You set the tone

When students see you laugh, sing on the bus, or stand wide-eyed in a museum — they follow your lead. They learn that learning is joyful. That wonder doesn’t stop when you grow up. That travel isn’t just a checklist — it’s a chance to feel something.

Your energy shapes the culture of the trip. And that starts with giving yourself permission to be more than just the organizer.

You’ve already done the work

By the time departure day arrives, you’ve already done the heavy lifting. With the right travel partner (hi, that’s us!), the logistics are in motion, the forms are in, the support team is activated.

Now? Your job is to lead with presence, not pressure.

You don’t have to solve every tiny hiccup alone. You don’t have to micromanage every meal or minute. You’ve built a team that has your back. Let them help you.

Take the moment

Take a moment to enjoy your coffee on the hotel patio. Take a moment to stand beside your students as they see the skyline for the first time. Take a moment to hear their laughter, their questions, their wow.

Because this trip isn’t just changing them — it’s reminding you why you do what you do.

Travel should be transformational — for you, too

At Impact Tours and Travel, I design every trip with one mission: to create something that’s meaningful for everyone involved. That means the students, yes — but also the teachers who made it all possible.

You deserve to come home tired — but fulfilled. To laugh. To learn. To lead with joy.

So, dear teacher…

Dance at the dinner cruise. Take the selfie. Sing the showtune. Stand in the middle of it all and soak it in.

You’ve earned this. And we’re here to make sure you enjoy every second of it.
