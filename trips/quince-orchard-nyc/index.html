! How Group Pricing Works — Please Read

Because this is a group trip, the price per student depends on how many students travel with us. Fixed costs, like the coach and the tour director, are shared across the whole group, so the more students who join, the lower the price for everyone.

If the group does not reach its projected enrollment, the price will adjust to match the actual number of registered students, and that adjusted price will be applied to every registration. This adjustment is finalized at least 30 days before the trip, on or about September 15, 2026. The best way to keep the price low is to encourage classmates to register early and stay enrolled.

Before You Register

A Few Important Notes

Please review these policies carefully before completing your registration below.

All Payments Are Non-Refundable

Your deposit is non-refundable from the moment it is paid — it holds airline seats and hotel rooms that are committed immediately. After that, what is refundable depends on how close to departure you cancel, on a published schedule that reaches zero 30 days out. See our Terms & Trip Policies for the full table, and register only when your family is ready to commit.

Travel Protection Available

Because payments are non-refundable, we strongly encourage families to consider travel insurance, which may be purchased through an independent third-party provider at checkout. Review the coverage carefully before deciding.

Pricing May Adjust

Your final price depends on total enrollment. If the group falls short of its projected size, the price will adjust to the matching tier and apply to every registration, finalized at least 30 days before the trip.

What Information We Collect

During registration we collect the information below, used only to register, support, and safely care for your student during the trip. We do not sell your information.

1 Participant Details

  • Student's full legal name and preferred name
  • Date of birth and grade or school
  • Home address
  • Student phone number and email (if applicable)

2 Parent & Emergency Contacts

  • Parent or guardian name, phone, and email
  • At least two emergency contacts
  • Relationship of each contact to the student

3 Health & Safety Information

  • Allergies, medical conditions, and current medications
  • Dietary needs and any accessibility accommodations
  • Physician contact and health insurance details
  • Used only to provide care; shared only with staff who need it

4 Forms & Authorizations

  • Signed permission and participation consent
  • Medical treatment authorization
  • Code of conduct agreement
  • Photo and video release (opt-out available)

5 Trip & Payment Selections

  • Trip selection and registration details
  • Payment and installment plan details
  • Card payments processed securely by a third-party processor

6 How It's Protected

  • Access limited to authorized trip staff and chaperones
  • Retained only as long as needed, then securely destroyed
  • Never sold or shared except to provide care or as required by law
