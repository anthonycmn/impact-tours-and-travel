A Look at the Trip

Your 5-Day Itinerary

Five days and four nights in New York City, March 2027. Times of day are approximate and may shift slightly based on show schedules. All five Broadway shows are to be determined and will be confirmed closer to the trip.

Day 1 · Thursday

Arrival, Times Square & an Evening Show

Fly in, settle into Times Square, and open the trip with a welcome dinner and your first Broadway performance.
Morning

Fly from Texas to New York

Group flight to NYC, arriving late morning, then a coach transfer to the Times Square hotel.

Afternoon

Check-in & Theater District Tour

Hotel check-in and welcome orientation, then a guided walk through Times Square, Schubert Alley, and the historic Drama Book Shop. Lunch on own along the way.

Evening

Welcome Dinner & Broadway Show #1

A welcome dinner at Havana Central in a private group room, then your first Broadway show, a stage door autograph experience, and the walk back to the hotel.

Day 2 · Friday

Morning Workshop & a Downtown Walk

Start with a Broadway workshop, then explore the city from Chinatown up to Times Square.
Morning

Broadway Workshop #1

Hotel breakfast, then a 90-minute session at the 36th & 8th studio with a professional Broadway artist covering song, movement, and acting. Subway south to Chinatown afterward.

Midday

Chinatown, SoHo & Greenwich Village

Explore Chinatown with an early lunch on own, then walk north through the SoHo arts district into Greenwich Village and Washington Square Park.

Afternoon

West Village, the Whitney & the Strand

Wander the West Village and Meatpacking District to the Whitney Museum of American Art (free under 25), then Union Square and the Strand Book Store before the subway back to Times Square.

Evening

Broadway Show #2

Dinner on own near the theaters, then your second Broadway show and a stage door autograph experience.

Day 3 · Saturday

Central Park, Radio City & Two Shows

An uptown morning, a Saturday matinee, the Museum of Broadway, and an evening performance.
Morning

Central Park, Lincoln Center & Radio City

Hotel breakfast, then a guided tour of Central Park and Lincoln Center, plus a Stage Door Tour of Radio City Music Hall on the way back.

Early Afternoon

Broadway Show #3 (Matinee)

Lunch on own, then the Saturday matinee with a stage door autograph experience afterward.

Late Afternoon

Museum of Broadway

Group admission to the Museum of Broadway, plus time at the Harry Potter New York flagship store before the evening show.

Evening

Broadway Show #4

Dinner on own near the theaters, then your fourth Broadway show and another stage door autograph experience.

Day 4 · Sunday

Second Workshop & a Farewell at Ellen's

A morning workshop, your final Broadway show, and the iconic singing-waiter dinner.
Morning

Garment District Walk

Hotel breakfast, then a guided stroll through the Garment District and the historic Drama Book Shop, leading right up to the workshop studio.

Midday

Broadway Workshop #2 & Grand Central

A 90-minute session covering stagecraft, choreography, and vocal performance, then lunch on own at Grand Central Terminal with a look at the Main Concourse and Whispering Gallery.

Afternoon

Broadway Show #5

Your final Broadway performance, with one last stage door meet and greet.

Evening

Farewell Dinner at Ellen's Stardust Diner

Celebrate the trip with the iconic Broadway singing-waiter experience back in the theater district.

Late Evening

Optional: Top of the Rock

An optional add-on to close out the trip with skyline views from the Top of the Rock observation deck at Rockefeller Center, just a short walk from dinner.

Day 5 · Monday

Departure Day

A few last NYC moments, then the flight home.
Morning

5th Avenue Walk & Airport Transfer

Hotel checkout and a grab-and-go breakfast, luggage stored if needed, then a guided walk along 5th Avenue and the Rockefeller area for last photos and souvenirs before the coach to the airport.

Afternoon

Return Flight to Texas

Fly home, arriving early evening.

Because this is a group trip, the price per person depends on how many travelers join us. Fixed costs, like the tour director and the group rates, are shared across the whole group, so the more travelers who join, the lower the price for everyone.

  • Student's full legal name (as it appears on a government-issued ID for air travel) and preferred name
